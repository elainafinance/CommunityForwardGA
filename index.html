What This Demo Can—and Can’t—Tell You

Community Forward Georgia is a public-information research and planning framework. Georgia Quick View and Deep Community Analysis organize public evidence about community conditions, institutions, investment, infrastructure, workforce, transportation, and potential opportunities. Community Forward does not rank or recommend real communities as data-center sites, establish technical capacity, determine environmental suitability, authorize incentives, provide legal or engineering conclusions, or represent community consent. Appropriate professional and local verification remains required.

The current public demonstration does not perform live internet research for newly entered communities. Unresearched locations demonstrate the research workflow and identify the evidence lanes that a production research service would run.
