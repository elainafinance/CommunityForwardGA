How Community Forward Georgia works

Start with the community. Follow the evidence.

A ZIP, city, or county becomes a wider picture of the municipality, county, utilities, schools, development systems, rail, and regional connections that shape local opportunity.

🌿

Core principle: Look for what a community has already built, funded, planned, lost, or possesses but does not fully use - then test whether major technology investment can unlock that capacity while satisfying genuine site requirements.

01

Community conditions first

Recent government, school, authority, financial and local records identify what a community is investing in, debating, building, losing or trying to fix. Only useful, sourced categories appear.

02

Current + future infrastructure

Planned, announced, funded, permitted, and under-construction projects remain visually and analytically separate from infrastructure operating today.

03

Evidence labels

Real-community evidence uses VERIFIED, REPORTED, INFERENCE, and RESEARCH NEEDED so public claims and analysis never become unsupported facts.

04

Professional gates

Power, water, environment, legal authority, incentives, railroad rights, site conditions, and community consent require the appropriate professional or local confirmation.

05

No opaque real-community score

Americus and other real communities are presented through evidence and open questions. Pinehaven retains synthetic score behavior only as a regression fixture.

06

Human review

No recommendation becomes validated because one research agent produced it. Sources, conflicts, stale information, unknowns, and review flags flow through the control layer.

07

Service geography, not ZIP silos

ZIP codes are entry points. Canonical findings may relate to several ZIPs through municipal, utility, school, rail, water, sewer, or economic relationships without being duplicated.

08

Cache-first research control

Saved geography and evidence records are reused when current. Agents report NOT_RUN, CACHED, UPDATED, NEW_RESEARCH_REQUIRED, or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE so unchanged work is not rerun.

09

Fiscal layers stay distinct

SPLOST and E-SPLOST are capital-need signals. LOST is an operating-revenue signal. Proprietary utility activity remains separate from ordinary General Fund revenue.

10

Project location is not capacity

A regional project may be relevant outside its host ZIP, but potential service-area impact stays separate from confirmed capacity and retains utility-engineering verification.

11

Georgia Quick View

A Georgia ZIP, city, or county shows the public finance, meeting, school, authority, utility, energy, capital, education, transportation and community-information lanes used in research. The static demo does not perform fresh live internet research. Project-specific gaps remain RESEARCH NEEDED and receive no forced score.

12

Ballot discovery is not validation

Ballotpedia is used for referendum discovery and historical context. Official ballot language, resolutions, election records or audited schedules are required before a measure is labeled VERIFIED.

Conditions become responsible options

The durability test

For each meaningful issue, the system separates the current condition, existing response, committed funding, remaining gap, possible major-investment response, durable ownership and recurring operating responsibility.

What is happening?

What is already being done?

What are residents already funding?

What remains unresolved?

What could major investment strengthen?

Who owns the result?

Who pays to operate it?

Would the benefit survive the developer?

Education infrastructure

Creation, not just consumption

The pathway now runs from elementary exposure through middle-school belonging and creation, CTAE, a separately funded 9th-grade bridge, later-grade dual enrollment, college or credentials, paid work-based learning, and local technology employment.

Controls

  • No youth marketing or consumer targeting
  • No claim that programs reduce crime or truancy without evidence
  • Do not present 9th-grade state-funded dual enrollment as available
  • Broadband and device access do not equal technology creation
  • Staffing, transport, maintenance and recurring funds are required
✓

Public-safe demonstration

Americus / Sumter County is the featured public deep analysis; Pinehaven is a fictional regression fixture. Neither Quick View nor Deep Analysis is a site recommendation or substitute for professional review. No capacity finding, incentive authorization, environmental conclusion, or claim of community consent is made.
